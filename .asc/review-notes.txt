RE 2.5.4 - BACKGROUND LOCATION
Persistent location is required by the live round session. When a round is started, Sticks keeps updating distance-to-green while the phone is locked and in the user's pocket, so that (a) the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity shows live yardage to the front/center/back of the green, and (b) the paired Apple Watch glance shows the same live yardage on the wrist. Significant-change and region monitoring are not sufficient: yardage must update continuously as the golfer walks, at meter-level accuracy. Background updates are enabled only for the duration of an active round and are turned off the moment the round ends.
Where to see it: Start Round > play a hole > lock the phone. The Live Activity yardage continues to update as you move. This is disclosed in-app on the round screen and in Settings > Apple Watch & Health.
A recording captured outdoors on a physical iPhone is attached (sticks_background_location.mp4): the round screen showing 157y to center, the phone then locked with Sticks backgrounded, and the Lock Screen Live Activity updating 148y > 141y > 136y as the tester walks. Note: iOS does not draw the background-location indicator on the Lock Screen - it appears in the status bar once the device is unlocked to the Home Screen with the round still active.

RE 2.5.1 - HEALTHKIT
HealthKit is used to save the golf round as a real workout: an HKWorkoutSession / HKLiveWorkoutBuilder on the Apple Watch collects heart rate and active energy and saves the finished round to the Health app, so it counts toward the user's Activity rings.
Where it is identified in the UI, on both devices:
1) Apple Watch - starting a round shows a sheet titled "Track this round as a Golf workout?" which states that it records heart rate, energy, and distance to Health and credits Activity rings. Nothing is recorded and no Health permission is requested until the user taps "Start Workout". "Not Now" declines, and an optional "Remember my choice" toggle sets the default.
2) Apple Watch round screen - a Health badge under the yardage identifies the feature at all times: it reads "TRACK IN HEALTH" when nothing is recording and "GOLF WORKOUT - HEALTH" while recording. The badge is a button: tap it to start or stop and save the workout, or to change the per-round default (Ask each round / Always track / Never track). The shipped default is "Ask each round".
3) iPhone - Settings > "Apple Watch & Health" explains that starting a round on the Watch records a golf workout to Apple Health with heart rate and active energy, and that the round is saved to Health when it ends.
The iPhone app itself does not read or write Health data; its only HealthKit call is HKHealthStore.startWatchApp(with:), which launches the Watch app for the round.

APP OVERVIEW
Tap Start Round to create a scorecard, enter scores per hole, and view the live leaderboard. The GPS tab shows hole yardages and uses location permission (grant when prompted). The Picks/Win % market is friendly play-money picks between players in the same group only - no real money, no deposits, no cash prizes, no gambling. Side games (Nassau, Skins, Snake) are score-tracking games with the same play-money disclaimer shown in-app.
