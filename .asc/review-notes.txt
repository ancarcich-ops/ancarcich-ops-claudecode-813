RE 2.5.4 - BACKGROUND LOCATION
The live round needs persistent location. With a round active, Sticks keeps updating distance-to-green while the phone is locked and pocketed, so the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity and the paired Watch both show live yardage to the front/center/back of the green. Significant-change and region monitoring cannot do this: yardage must update continuously as the golfer walks, at meter accuracy. Updates run only during a round.
To see it: Start Round > play a hole > lock the phone; the yardage keeps updating as you move. Disclosed on the round screen and in Settings > Apple Watch & Health.
Evidence - PART 1 of the attachment (sticks_review_evidence.mp4), filmed outdoors on a physical iPhone:
0:03-0:46 - round screen at 157y to center; phone locked with Sticks backgrounded, the Lock Screen Live Activity updates 148y > 141y > 136y as the tester walks. Only a live location stream can move those numbers.

Note: iOS does not draw the location indicator on the Lock Screen, and on a Dynamic Island iPhone it is not in the status bar - Control Center is where it shows.

RE 2.5.1 - HEALTHKIT
The round is saved as a real workout: an HKWorkoutSession / HKLiveWorkoutBuilder on the Watch collects heart rate and active energy and saves the round to Health, crediting Activity rings. Identified in the UI on both devices:
1) Watch - starting a round shows a sheet, "Track this round as a Golf workout?", stating it records heart rate, energy and distance to Health and credits Activity rings. Nothing is recorded and no Health prompt appears until "Start Workout" is tapped. The decline button reads "Not Now", or "Never Track" with "Remember my choice" on. Default is "Ask each round".
2) Watch round screen - a Health chip sits in the top row next to the course name, no scrolling needed: "HEALTH" when idle, "REC - HEALTH" in red while recording. Tap it to start, to stop and save, or to change the default. The watch home screen shows a "GOLF WORKOUT - HEALTH" chip even with no round active.
3) iPhone - Settings > "Apple Watch & Health" explains the same. The iPhone never reads or writes Health data; its only HealthKit call is startWatchApp(with:).

APP OVERVIEW
Start Round creates a scorecard; enter scores per hole and see the live leaderboard. The GPS tab shows hole yardages (grant location when prompted). The Picks/Win % market is play-money picks between players in the same group - no real money, deposits, cash prizes or gambling. Side games (Nassau, Skins, Snake) are score-tracking only.
