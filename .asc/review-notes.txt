RE 2.5.4 - BACKGROUND LOCATION
Persistent location is required by the live round session. With a round active, Sticks keeps updating distance-to-green while the phone is locked and pocketed, so (a) the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity shows live yardage to the front/center/back of the green and (b) the paired Apple Watch glance shows the same yardage on the wrist. Significant-change and region monitoring are not sufficient: yardage must update continuously as the golfer walks, at meter-level accuracy. Background updates are on only for the duration of a round and stop the moment it ends.
Where to see it: Start Round > play a hole > lock the phone; the Live Activity yardage keeps updating as you move. Disclosed in-app on the round screen and in Settings > Apple Watch & Health.
Evidence captured outdoors on a physical iPhone is attached (sticks_location_evidence.mp4, 53s):
0:00-0:43 - the round screen showing 157y to center; the phone is then locked with Sticks backgrounded and the Lock Screen Live Activity updates 148y > 141y > 136y as the tester walks. Only a live location stream can move those numbers with the app backgrounded.
0:43-0:48 - Control Center during that same active round: the header shows the blue location arrow with "Sticks" (here "Rivian & Sticks", alongside another app), and the arrow also appears in the status bar.
0:48-0:53 - the Privacy panel reached by tapping that header: "Sticks - Location", meaning currently active, as distinct from the "Location, recently" listed for an app that has stopped.
Note on the indicator: iOS does not draw it on the Lock Screen, and on a Dynamic Island iPhone it is not visible in the Home Screen status bar - Control Center is where it can be seen.

RE 2.5.1 - HEALTHKIT
HealthKit saves the golf round as a real workout: an HKWorkoutSession / HKLiveWorkoutBuilder on the Apple Watch collects heart rate and active energy and saves the finished round to Health, crediting Activity rings.
Where it is identified in the UI, on both devices:
1) Apple Watch - starting a round shows a sheet titled "Track this round as a Golf workout?" stating it records heart rate, energy, and distance to Health and credits Activity rings. Nothing is recorded and no Health permission is requested until the user taps "Start Workout". "Not Now" declines; an optional "Remember my choice" toggle sets the default.
2) Apple Watch round screen - a Health badge under the yardage identifies the feature at all times: "TRACK IN HEALTH" when idle, "GOLF WORKOUT - HEALTH" while recording. It is a button: tap to start, or to stop and save, or to change the default (Ask each round / Always track / Never track). Shipped default is "Ask each round".
3) iPhone - Settings > "Apple Watch & Health" explains that starting a round on the Watch records a golf workout to Apple Health with heart rate and active energy, saved when the round ends.
The iPhone app itself does not read or write Health data; its only HealthKit call is HKHealthStore.startWatchApp(with:), which launches the Watch app for the round.

APP OVERVIEW
Tap Start Round to create a scorecard, enter scores per hole, and view the live leaderboard. The GPS tab shows hole yardages (grant location when prompted). The Picks/Win % market is friendly play-money picks between players in the same group only - no real money, no deposits, no cash prizes, no gambling. Side games (Nassau, Skins, Snake) are score-tracking games with the same play-money disclaimer shown in-app.
